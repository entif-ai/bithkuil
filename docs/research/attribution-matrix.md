# Attribution and result gradients

The integrated trial is first. These tests identify what survives a result; they are not gates that prevent SYS from starting after its own engineering acceptance.

## H01: Semantic reconstruction tax

Test: SYS and matched replay. Critical control: Competent CNL; common world information; full cost ledger.

Positive update: Persistent efficiency gain under appropriate controls. Downward update: No gain across competent matched conditions weakens this operationalization. Local survival: H03 generic factorization may survive donor loss.

## H02: Representation efficiency

Test: Equal-exposure and independently compute-matched frontiers. Critical control: Token length; vocab; padding; teacher cost.

Positive update: Better quality/cost frontier. Downward update: Gain vanishes after denominator correction. Local survival: Compact codec can remain useful without learner gain.

## H03: Factored semantics

Test: B versus nonseparable mixing and generic AST. Critical control: Information invertibility; covariance; vocabulary.

Positive update: Aligned reusable factors improve held-out composition. Downward update: No advantage over mixing or opaque controls. Local survival: H19 donor specificity distinct.

## H04: Grammar before lexicon

Test: Grammar-first versus lexicon-first schedule. Critical control: Matched exposures and tasks.

Positive update: Better transfer with grammar-first order. Downward update: No benefit or delayed useful competence. Local survival: Other curricula can survive.

## H05: Prerequisite topology

Test: Topology versus difficulty-only; shuffled DAG. Critical control: Same eligible content and assistance.

Positive update: Transfer/retention improves. Downward update: Wrong DAG or no benefit after schedule matching. Local survival: Within-object order remains separate.

## H06: Visible subsymbol structure

Test: Stable decomposition versus opaque joint code. Critical control: Token inventory and atom coverage.

Positive update: Unseen composition advantage. Downward update: Only shorter sequences help. Local survival: Compression claim remains distinct.

## H07: Within-object traversal

Test: Predeclared dependency traversal versus legal reorderings. Critical control: Same AST semantics and cross-example curriculum.

Positive update: Faster/robuster composition. Downward update: Order effect disappears under length/data control. Local survival: H05 cross-example ordering may survive.

## H08: Compositional supervision

Test: Factored auxiliary targets versus answer-only. Critical control: Extra supervision budget and teacher access.

Positive update: Transfer improves at matched total supervision cost. Downward update: Only extra labels explain gain. Local survival: Answer-only system finding survives.

## H09: Precision interaction

Test: Representation by ternary/full-precision factorial. Critical control: Same architecture; native kernels separately measured.

Positive update: Representation moderates precision penalty. Downward update: No interaction or regression. Local survival: Representation main effect can survive.

## H10: Cognitive algebra

Test: Nonlinguistic exact operators and held-out compositions. Critical control: Independent oracle specification; world-sensitive pairs.

Positive update: Generalizes beyond lexical cues. Downward update: Only templates memorized. Local survival: Useful formal infrastructure survives.

## H11: Later natural-language transfer

Test: Frozen developmental seed plus held-out language codec. Critical control: Full LM head; parser errors; equal transfer budget.

Positive update: Prior learning transfers. Downward update: Transfer costs erase advantage. Local survival: Synthetic domain utility may survive.

## H12: Working-state/context economics

Test: Matched semantic states at fixed context budget. Critical control: Information loss and retrieval cost.

Positive update: Less context for same correct decisions. Downward update: Savings require dropping needed information. Local survival: Other efficiency outcomes unaffected.

## H13: Developmental teacher

Test: Adaptive validated proposals versus deterministic-only. Critical control: Equal privileges; no gold leakage.

Positive update: Net accepted-competence benefit after teacher cost. Downward update: Teacher overhead or leakage explains benefit. Local survival: Representation-only result remains.

## H14: Generator-family generalization

Test: Custodian sealed family implementations. Critical control: Unseen implementations; isomorphism/template audit.

Positive update: Transfers across declared shifts. Downward update: Exact/structural leakage or family collapse. Local survival: In-domain learning may remain.

## H15: Integrated systems demonstration

Test: SYS-B versus credible SYS-C. Critical control: Shared ternary architecture and competent CNL.

Positive update: Predeclared +5pp practical signal and interval. Downward update: Competent baseline with no meaningful benefit. Local survival: Attribute local failures rather than all-or-nothing theory.

## H16: Orders-of-magnitude upside

Test: Multiscale fixed-quality total-cost frontier. Critical control: Amortization; human/teacher/compiler denominators.

Positive update: Large replicated total-cost improvements. Downward update: Moderate/no savings at larger scales. Local survival: Smaller practical gain remains.

## H17: Contamination control

Test: Exact and structural split audits; forbidden-help fixtures. Critical control: Independent custody and immutable logs.

Positive update: Clean boundary with valid tests. Downward update: Any sealed influence invalidates confirmatory claim. Local survival: Engineering repairs remain useful.

## H18: Capability routing

Test: Local-pedagogue and matched assistance ledger. Critical control: Exact model/version; rejection and retry accounting.

Positive update: Low-cost accepted help without hidden asymmetry. Downward update: Escalation/declassification creates unmeasured advantage. Local survival: Research authoring value is separate.

## H19: Ithkuil donor specificity

Test: B versus equally engineered generic factored schema. Critical control: Alphabet-renaming symmetry and equal author effort.

Positive update: Donor selection/scoping beats generic schema. Downward update: Generic schema matches or wins. Local survival: H01/H03 may be supported while H19 fails.

## H20: Formal truth boundary

Test: Dual oracle, injected wrong proposals, malformed ABI. Critical control: Independent semantics review; no LLM gold authority.

Positive update: Bad proposals rejected and exact domains check. Downward update: Oracle discrepancy or semantic misspecification. Local survival: Truth checker repair does not prove ML benefit.
