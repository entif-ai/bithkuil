# Deviations and analysis epochs

Create an append-only deviation record with ID, discovery time, affected run/condition/seed, prior plan hash, observed fact, exact change, reason, whether sealed outcomes were visible, and disposition. Never rewrite the original plan hash. Mechanical repairs before sealed access may retain confirmatory intent only after independently checking that the estimand and treatment remain unchanged; otherwise begin a new study epoch.

After unblinding, treat altered thresholds, family filters, exclusion rules, budgets, seed counts, endpoint definitions and model assistance as exploratory until independently confirmed. Failed seeds stay in the inventory. Recovery from a valid same-config checkpoint is not a new seed. Hardware changes are recorded even when mathematically intended to be equivalent.
